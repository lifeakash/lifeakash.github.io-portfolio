Questions clients ask before hiring an Android developer

How much does it cost to develop an Android app?

It depends on the features, number of screens, backend needs and design. Send me a short brief and I will give you a fixed quote, so you know the price before any work starts.

How long does it take to build an Android app?

A focused first version usually takes a few weeks. Apps with user accounts, payments or a custom backend take longer. Your quote includes a timeline.

Can you publish the app on Google Play for me?

Yes. I handle Play Console setup, app signing, the store listing, screenshots and release, and I can optimise the listing so people can find your app.

Can you fix or take over an existing Android app?

Yes. I fix crashes, upgrade apps to the latest Android target API, add features and clean up code started by another developer.

Do I own the source code?

Yes. After final payment, the full source code and store assets are yours.

Do you also build websites?

Yes. I build fast, mobile-first, SEO-ready websites and web apps, so one developer can deliver both your app and your site.
